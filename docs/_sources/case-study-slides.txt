The highway operator company keeps records of the current speed limit, weather, lighting conditions and accident history (including near accidents). 
Using machine learning, it wants to explore the relationship between those data. In production, the neural network shall only be used if it is dark or the weather is bad. Under those conditions it shall decide by what amount to reduce the speed limit. 
Bad weather and darkness are not the only possible reasons to reduce the speed limit. Another reason is bad air quality. Here the company has no sufficient data for machine learning. So an LLM should decide based on the current air quality (queried via API) if and by how much the speed limit should be reduced.
If it is not dark and both the weather and the air quality are good, the speed limit is not reduced at all. It remains at 80 km/h.
