These are the risks that matter most. The prototype handles most of them itself: the guard, the honest flag, the fallback table, and licence plates are never even loaded. On the target we made the call ourselves: the system keeps the requirement and goes as low as it can, showing honestly when the target is out of reach. For production, a full year of data would be needed, and a controlled trial on one section would be the optimal way to measure what the speed limit really does.
