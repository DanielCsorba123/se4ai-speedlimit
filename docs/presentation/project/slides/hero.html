SpeedLimit MVP · 6 · Figure F04 · 744 hours, January

This is the most important slide. Every bar is a speed limit under a condition, and every bar is above the red line of one near-accident per hour. The correlation between limit and incidents is practically zero. Worse, in the dark the lowest limits show the most incidents. That is not because slow driving is dangerous; operators lowered the limit when the night was already bad. History mixes up cause and effect, and no model can untangle that from one month of data.
