SpeedLimit MVP · 2

The highway operator wants the speed limit set automatically. Three rules. When it is dark or icy, a neural network decides how far to reduce, with a target of at most one near-accident per hour. When poor air quality is forecast, a language model decides. Otherwise the limit stays at 80. We were asked to build a working prototype of all of this.
