Let me show you how an operator uses it. A normal day gives eighty. Set the light to night and the network decides, here seventy, and it tells us honestly that the target is not reachable. Black ice in daylight works the same way. Exactly one thousand micrometres of water is not ice, because the rule says more than one thousand. For poor air the language model decides live, here fifty, and when night and very poor air come together, the strictest reason wins at fifty.
